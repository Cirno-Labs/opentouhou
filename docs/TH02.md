# th02

Notes on how 東方封魔録 ~ the Story of Eastern Wonderland is structured and
how its parts map onto opentouhou. The overall design is in
[ARCHITECTURE.md](ARCHITECTURE.md), and the PC-98 hardware it models is in
[PC98.md](PC98.md).

## Files

The original's files, as the port reads them. Original files are only ever
read; what the port saves goes to the user directory. The sprites that
the original compiles into its programs (`bombpart.bmp`, `pellet.bmp`,
`pointnum.bmp`, `sparks.bmp`, `verdict.bmp`) are built into the port.

`TOUHOU.DAT` holds the logo's sprites. Everything else sits in the
`東方封魔.録` archive, whose entries are XOR-keyed with 12h and optionally
run-length packed. The port never reads the original's programs.

Scores and settings:

- `HUUHI.DAT`: the high scores, five sections of ten places, one per
  difficulty and Extra, each with checksums, and the clear markers that
  unlock Extra. The port saves its own copy in the user directory.
- `huuma.cfg`: the options in eight bytes: difficulty, music mode, bombs,
  spare lives, the reduced-effects flag and three unused bytes. The port
  keeps its options in `config.ini` and imports `huuma.cfg` once, when the
  user directory has no `config.ini`.

Stages:

- `stage0.dt1` to `stage5.dt1`: a stage's scroll order of map sections,
  its enemy formations and their scripts.
- `stage0.map` to `stage5.map`: a stage's background, in sections of tile
  numbers.
- `stage0.mpn` to `stage5.mpn`: the 16x16 tiles that the map is laid out
  from, with their palette.
- `stage0.bft` to `stage5.bft`, `.bmt`, `.bbt`: a stage's sprite banks, in
  BFNT format.
- `stage3_b.btt`, `stage5b1.bft`, `stage5b2.bft`, `mima.bft`,
  `mima1.bft`, `mima2.bft`: the bosses' extra sprite banks.
- `stage0.txt` to `stage5.txt`: the dialogue, in rows of 80 bytes of text
  RAM.
- `eye.pi`: the stages' backdrop picture.

Gameplay:

- `miko.bft`, `miko32.bft`, `miko16.bft`: the gameplay sprites, in cels
  of 32x48, 32x32 and 16x16.
- `mikoft.bft`: the glyphs loaded as gaiji.
- `miko_k.mpn`: the dialogue faces, as tiles.
- `bombs.bft`, `bomb1.bft`, `bomb1.pi` to `bomb3.pi`: the bombs'
  transition, text and illustrations.
- `demo1.rec` to `demo3.rec`: the title screen's recorded demos.

Opening and ending:

- `op.pi`, `op2.pi`, `op3.pi`, `opa.pi` to `opc.pi`, `op.rgb`, `op_h.bft`,
  `op_h.rgb`: the title, the menus and their palettes.
- `tselect.pi`, `ts1.pi` to `ts3.pi`: the shot type select.
- `music.txt`: the music room's comments.
- `end1.txt`, `end2.txt`: the ending's stories; `end3.txt`: the verdict
  screen's titles; `endft.bft`: the ending's title sprites.
- `ed01.pi` to `ed09.pi`, `ed*.rgb`, `all.pi`, `but.pi`, `extra.pi`: the
  ending's pictures and their palettes.

Music and sound:

- `op.m`, `stage0.m` to `stage5.m`, `boss1.m` to `boss5.m`, `mima.m`,
  `end1.m`, `ending.m`: the music, as PMD data.
- `huuma.efc`: the sound effects, as PMD effect data.
