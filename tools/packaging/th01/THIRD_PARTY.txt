OPNA emulation
--------------

The C cores and required headers in src/pc98/opna/vendor come from
libvgm, revision c5c03a2c2514d38d2ab7b7e8dbd021af0b8292cb:
https://github.com/ValleyBell/libvgm

fmopn.c declares GPL-2.0-or-later and credits Jarek Burczynski and
Tatsuyuki Satoh. ay8910.c declares BSD-3-Clause and credits Couriersud.
See COPYING.GPL-2 and COPYING.BSD-3-Clause. Their texts apply to the
individual declarations, not the whole import.

ymdeltat.c credits Hiromitsu Shioya, Tatsuyuki Satoh and Jarek Burczynski
without an explicit license tag in this revision. Its MAME lineage
declares GPL-2.0-or-later:
https://github.com/mamedev/mame/blob/mame0220/src/devices/sound/ymdeltat.cpp
The small interface, type and logging files also have no explicit
per-file license tags in this revision. This records their provenance;
it does not establish blanket license clearance for the import.

fmopn_2608rom.h documents a rhythm table reconstructed from chip output
and verified on hardware. It contains chip rhythm data, not original
game audio. Our synthetic-register tests do not establish hardware or
original playback parity.

Local adaptations use caller-owned storage, remove unused heap and
external sample-memory interfaces, expose SSG DAC contributions, freeze
the rhythm lookup after setup and use native divider steps. Rhythm
prescaler handling and signed/unsigned scaling are adapted for the
native backend. Individual source notices remain in the source tree.
